# In-person briefing notes (2026-11-07, ~11:26)

Raw team notes from the organizers' oral briefing, cleaned up. Official PDFs override these if they conflict.

## What the business wants
- Evaluate customer conversations for **quality control**, and keep improving through customer feedback.
- Analytics and evaluation of **real conversations**, from bot conversations through to live human operators.
- Use the errors found to decide **where to develop and improve next**.
- **One manual check isn't enough**: dialog volume is large, and people read the metrics differently. The business needs **regular analysis** plus a tool that does it.

## Trust problem
- People don't trust AI and want a real specialist. We need a way to make customers feel calmer about it.
- If the bot can't answer after it has established contact with the customer, it **hands off to a real support operator**. The handoff should feel humanlike, talkative, with good communication.

## Deliverable framing
- Simulate the whole process: a **quality-control prototype**. Evaluate the dialog, the interface, and the **scoring formula**.
- We will get a sample set. Judges evaluate the product on **their own personal sample**.
- **Stop-code** is announced; the final product is checked as a whole. Control data comes **1 hour before stop-code**.
- Student guide: `brief.md`. Checklist: `checklist.md`. Judging criteria: **TBD, will be sent later** → save to `judging.md`.
